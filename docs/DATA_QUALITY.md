# Data quality report

Written 15 Nov 2026 from the code on branch `enhance/rag-hardening`. It lists every data trap we found in the client package and how the app handles each one.

The traps come from Section 7 of `KIRO_ENHANCEMENT_PLAN.md` (the Week 1 audit) plus the ones we found while building the eval suite. They are described in words only: no client values, no employee names and no employee codes. The "Evidence" column names the eval case (see `docs/QUESTION_CATALOGUE.md`) or the test file that covers the trap. Assumption IDs (A1 and so on) refer to `docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md`.

## Traps and how the app handles them

| # | Trap | How the app handles it | Where | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Severity scale flips on 1 Jul 2025.** Before that date 1 is worst; from that date 5 is worst. Counting "severity 1" across all years mixes the worst and the least severe incidents. | Each incident keeps its raw value and its scale. Code also converts to one scale where 5 is worst (A1). Worst-case counts use the converted value. Answers carry a note when the evidence spans the change and say the mapping is not confirmed. | `jm_rag/sources_mysql.py`, `jm_rag/facts.py`, `jm_rag/notes.py` | I1, I2, I3; `tests/test_validator.py`, `tests/test_facts.py` |
| 2 | **Sensors cover only five machines** (CNC-01, CNC-02, CNC-03, CMP-01, PMP-T01), for the first half of 2026. | A sensor question about another machine abstains and names the machines that do have sensor data. The covered list is read from the data, not hard-coded. | `jm_rag/retrieval.py` (`plan_query`), `jm_rag/pipeline.py` | V4, V7; `tests/test_fixes_4b.py` |
| 3 | **Some temperatures were logged in Fahrenheit**, and the sensor file uses **two timestamp formats** (ISO text and epoch milliseconds). | The loader converts Fahrenheit to Celsius and both timestamp formats to UTC, and keeps the original value, unit and format. ISO timestamps with no timezone are rejected, not guessed. Duplicate timestamps keep the first row and are flagged. Answers use the normalised values; a Fahrenheit question gets values converted by code from Celsius and says the data is stored in Celsius. | `scripts/sensor_csv_to_mysql.py`, `jm_rag/facts.py` | V9; `tests/test_condition_check.py` |
| 4 | **Gaps in the sensor readings.** | Gaps are recorded in `sensor_gaps` and summarised. Missing readings are not interpolated; the Sensor Trends tab says so. | `scripts/sensor_csv_to_mysql.py`, `app.py` (Sensor Trends) | V8 (tier 3, see `docs/EVAL_RESULTS.md`) |
| 5 | **The data ends on 30 Jun 2026.** Questions about later dates, forecasts, or "this year" and "last year" need a fixed reference point. | Anything after the end date abstains with a note. Relative periods are read against the end of the data: this year is 2026, last year is 2025, this month is June 2026, last month is May 2026 (A14). Every computed-facts source states the end date, and code requires it in answers about open or recent items. | `jm_rag/config.py` (`DATA_END`), `jm_rag/dates.py`, `jm_rag/key_figures.py` | V5, W1, A1, A2; `tests/test_phase5_fixes.py` |
| 6 | **CNC-02 vibration and temperature rise** during the sensor period, by day and by night, while CNC-01 and CNC-03 stay flat. The data cannot say why. | Code finds the month the rise starts and requires it in the answer. "Why" and "is it about to fail" questions abstain on the cause, with a note that the data holds no causes, alarm limits or failure history. The validator rejects condition judgements such as "in good shape", even negated. | `jm_rag/facts.py` (`sensor_facts`), `jm_rag/notes.py`, `jm_rag/validator.py` | V1, V2, V3, W5, X3, A8, Q16; `tests/test_condition_check.py` |
| 7 | **Shift hours are not recorded.** | Day and night are assumed (A4) and the assumption is written into the shift source. Without the shift source, a shift question abstains. | `jm_rag/sources_mysql.py`, `jm_rag/sensors.py`, `jm_rag/gaps.py` | V2, A8; `tests/test_phase5_fixes.py` |
| 8 | **The spares list has an entry for machine `ALL`**, and the nameplates read more than one lubricant grade, so one oil cannot fit every machine. | `ALL` is never expanded to machines (A2). A lubricant answer shows a conflict between the machine's plate and the `ALL` row. A machine's spares answer shows the `ALL` row as unresolved. | `jm_rag/conflicts.py` | N1, N3, S4, S5, S7, M2, M3, Q14 |
| 9 | **Spares lead times are free text**, not a number of days. | Lead times are shown as written and never summed or compared. | `jm_rag/sources_files.py`, `jm_rag/facts.py` | S2 |
| 10 | **The spares workbook states no currency.** | SGD is assumed and the answer says so (A5). | `jm_rag/facts.py`, `jm_rag/key_figures.py` | S6; `tests/test_key_figures.py` |
| 11 | **Implausible nameplate capacity readings**: some plates give a capacity in a unit that does not fit the machine type, and a crane plate carries no tonnage. | The OCR import flags these readings (`SUSPECT_CAPACITY_UNIT`, `SUSPECT_WELDER_CAPACITY`). A capacity question does not report a flagged reading as the capacity and abstains, unless an SOP gives the load. For the crane, the SOP load is shown beside the plate as a conflict, and the SOP is trusted. | `scripts/nameplate_ocr_import.py`, `jm_rag/gaps.py`, `jm_rag/conflicts.py` | N8, N9; `tests/test_validator.py` |
| 12 | **No 5-Why or investigation documents** are in the package, although some forms say one is attached. | Such a request abstains with "No 5-Why, root-cause or investigation document is in the data, even where a form says one is attached." | `jm_rag/guardrails/patterns.py`, `jm_rag/notes.py` | I10, A6 |
| 13 | **An SOP mentions a sling near-miss that no incident record matches** in that month. | Code reports the SOP statement as written, flags the gap in the incident log as a conflict, and does not invent an incident. | `jm_rag/conflicts_records.py` (`_sop_event_not_in_records`) | I12, Q13 |
| 14 | **OCR is unreliable.** Measured accuracy against the team's checked readings: 58% of structured nameplate fields, 71% of raw-text readings next to a label, 81% of incident-form fields (A18). | Every OCR value is labelled unverified unless a person approved it in the OCR Review tab (A19). Answers that use OCR carry a note. Raw OCR, reviewed OCR and MySQL are cited as separate sources. Eval cases that fail on wrong OCR are labelled "OCR data error, fix via OCR Review" and are not re-baselined. | `scripts/ocr_review.py`, `jm_rag/notes.py`, `scripts/ocr_accuracy_report.py` | N4, N6, N10 (OCR data errors); `tests/test_phase5_fixes.py` |
| 15 | **The structured OCR field is sometimes empty while the raw OCR text holds the value** next to its label. | The raw reading is reported, marked unverified, with the raw text as a cited source. | `jm_rag/gaps.py` (`plate_field_gaps`) | N2 |
| 16 | **One incident appears in several sources** (CMMS, OCR staging of the form, processed OCR), sometimes with different values. | The versions are grouped and counted once. The CMMS record is treated as authoritative; differences are shown as a conflict. | `jm_rag/incidents.py`, `jm_rag/conflicts_records.py` | I7, I11 |
| 17 | **Some incidents are recorded as near misses but have lost days.** | Code lists them and shows a conflict. | `jm_rag/conflicts_records.py`, `jm_rag/facts.py` | I8 |
| 18 | **Partial dates** such as "26 July" in a work-order question. | The app asks which year and does not query until it knows. A bare year as the next message completes the date. Impossible dates and reversed ranges also get a clarifying question. | `jm_rag/dates.py`, `jm_rag/pipeline.py` | `tests/test_characterization.py`, `tests/test_fixes_4b.py`, `tests/test_pipeline.py` |
| 19 | **Ambiguous machine words** such as "the crane" or "the lathe" match more than one machine. | The app asks which machine and gives the IDs. | `jm_rag/pipeline.py`, `jm_rag/tags.py` | A7, Q7 |
| 20 | **Unknown machine IDs and report numbers.** | The app says it could not find that machine or report and lists the known machines. | `jm_rag/pipeline.py` (`_not_found_statements`) | `tests/test_fixes_4b.py` |
| 21 | **Possible duplicate people**: some employee codes share a full name. | Flagged, never merged, and no name is shown (A9). | `jm_rag/gaps.py`, `jm_rag/sources_mysql.py` | G7 |
| 22 | **Personal data in the records**: employee names, the OCR reviewer field, and free text on incident forms. | Names never reach the model or the user: the input guard refuses name requests, sources are sanitised, and the output guard checks the final text (A3, A20). | `jm_rag/guardrails/` | G1, G2, G3, G8; hostile set (40 prompts); `tests/test_guardrails.py` |
| 23 | **Images without an OCR record.** | They are listed as "image awaiting OCR review" and their contents are not interpreted. | `jm_rag/sources_ocr.py` | `tests/test_source_loading.py` |
| 24 | **No maintenance schedule** is in the data. | "When is the next service due?" abstains (A15). | `jm_rag/notes.py` | Q12 |

## What we would confirm with the client
